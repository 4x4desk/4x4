# How we work

This page is for anyone about to contribute, and especially for an AI agent. Read it before you change anything.

## Current thinking, not rules

What's written here is current thinking: what we lean toward today, why, what it costs, and what would change our mind. Writing a direction down as "decided" or "never" turns a best guess into a wall nobody questions. Keep "must" for facts: a release must be signed.

## Options stay warm

When you propose a choice, give the realistic options with their costs, your recommendation first with its reason. Don't bury the alternatives: one set aside today may come back when its reason no longer holds.

## Check before claiming

Say how you know: you ran it, you saw it on screen, you read it in the code, the vendor's own page says so, or it's a guess. "I don't know" beats a confident guess. Read the source yourself before you build on a summary of it.

## A person, not a bot

4x4 acts only for the person at the computer, in their own session, at their word. Keep words and designs clear of anything that reads as automated collection from other people's sites.

## No time estimates

Don't say how long something will take, or call work quick or small. Describe what it involves: its parts, what's unknown, where it could fail.

## Finish before switching

A new idea that comes up mid-work gets written down in one line, and the work in hand gets finished first.
