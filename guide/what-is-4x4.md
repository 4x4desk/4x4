# What 4x4 is

4x4 is a desktop for people who work at large monitors. The screen is divided into zones, each zone shows one app at a time, and a workspace says which apps sit where. Switching workspaces changes the whole screen to the tools for another job.

Every app is one folder of plain files. The person can read and change it, and so can their own AI agent. 4x4 runs on the person's own computer, and what they store stays there.

## Who it's for

People who work all day at a desktop computer with big screens and want their tools arranged for each job, and kept that way, with their own AI to help reshape them. And builders, human or AI, who want to make an app that does one thing well.

## The levels

4x4 comes in levels. Each builds on the one before, and the same words, zones and look carry across all of them.

| Level | What the person gets | Today |
|---|---|---|
| 4x4 website | [4x4desk.app](https://4x4desk.app), where a person first meets 4x4 | Live |
| 4x4 2WD Desk | A lighter desk installed from Chrome, with a companion extension | An early version exists |
| 4x4 AWD Desk | The full desk, a browser of its own, for Mac, Windows and Linux | In alpha; most of the work happens here |
| 4x4 OS | The whole computer on Linux, beside the system already there or in its place | Not built yet |

This repository holds the open parts of 4x4 AWD Desk that are ready for someone new to read. Next: [the layers](layers.md).
